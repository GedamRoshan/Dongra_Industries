Company Overview

Crafting Everyday Cleaning Excellence Since Day One

Dongra Industries was founded with a clear purpose: high-performance, stain-fighting cleaning solutions should be dependable, skin-friendly, and accessible to every household.

Under our flagship brand PRITE, we develop laundry detergent powders, concentrated dishwashing gels, and institutional cleaning solutions that combine superior grease-cutting power with gentle care for fabrics and hands.
