What Happens Inside
Your Innerwear?

Your innerwear sits closest to your body — all day, every day. That warm, moist zone between fabric and skin is not just uncomfortable. It quietly becomes the perfect breeding ground for bacteria and fungi. Scientists call it a "microclimate."

What is a Microclimate?

A microclimate is the small zone of warmth and moisture that forms between your innerwear and skin. Body heat raises local temperature to 35–37°C. Sweat and natural discharge add moisture. Poor air circulation traps both.
